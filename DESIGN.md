# Codex LightCut visual direction

Audience: creators who want Codex to prepare an editable Jianying draft while they continue working.
Primary job: explain the project, distinguish macOS and Windows readiness, and direct readers to a useful next step.

- Ink `#0D1117`: page background, matching the supplied GitHub reference.
- Graphite `#171D25`: hero and timeline surfaces.
- Line `#303A46`: frames and quiet separators.
- Paper `#F2F5F9`: headline and platform cards.
- Steel `#A9B6C7`: secondary text.
- Ice `#C1DCD8`: restrained timeline selection and progress marker.

Display: system heavy sans, with PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei for Chinese. Body: system sans. Utility: monospace timecodes. No external fonts or trackers.

Layout: a wide dark hero above two light platform cards, then compact language/navigation links. Documentation continues as readable Markdown. The companion static page follows the same hierarchy, stacks on small screens, and uses real links rather than unavailable download buttons.

Signature: a slim editable timeline running beneath the headline. Separate video, caption and sound strips make the deliverable recognizable without depicting an unbuilt application UI. The diagonal gap in the LC mark repeats the cut between timeline clips.

Critique: generic orbital decorations from the reference would say little about editing; replace them with the timeline. Keep the large typography and platform contrast. No Linux card, fake installer, unsupported speed multiplier or full-feature claim.
